Features that would encourage women to use the app:

- Holistic health tracking with equal importance to periods as much as things like sleep, calorie counting, etc.
- Meaningful insights about cycle data (not your normal how long last cycle was, etc). Eg, Workout suggestions on the basis of the phase of the cycle you are in.
- Data security, such private data shouldn’t be collected by the app
  - https://sflc.in/menstruapps-tracking-your-cycle-or-tracking-you/
  - https://www.thebureauinvestigates.com/stories/2025-09-03/meta-was-caught-eavesdropping-on-a-period-app-could-this-be-the-start-of-a-pushback-against-big-tech
- Collected data insights. Let’s say I logged a particular symptom or a late period then some data correlation of all women in my demographic(age, location, etc) to identify if things like geography and weather have a mass effect on women. Especially with climate change external conditions might have an impact on menstrual health of women. - Not sure how we could do this without collecting data though, probably anonymously collecting data?
- Finding good gynecologists around me.
- Ability to export my period data in a report so I can share it with a gynecologist directly.
- Opt-in nudge notifications which see your daily activity and phone usage and ask you to log symptoms like mood, etc.
- Entering period data manually can be tedious, ability to speak with the app to get it to log my cycle, symptoms, etc.
- Ability to share period information with your partner/family member/gynac in a secure way. Could help the other person to better anticipate your mood, could help couples who are trying to conceive.
- Ability to migrate period data from another app and vice versa.
